03The Outback

2026 Outback. Fully redesigned.

The Outback is on its seventh generation for 2026, and this is the biggest change the nameplate has seen in years.

The old wagon shape is gone. The new one is taller and squarer, closer to what people picture when they think "SUV." If you loved the old one, you'll need to come sit in the new one before deciding. We've had longtime Outback owners walk in skeptical and walk out converted, and we've had a few who decided the previous gen on our pre owned lot was still their car. Both are valid.

Two engines, two personalities

  • 2.5L Boxer, 180 hp, ~27 mpg combined, the everyday Outback, and honestly the one most of our buyers go home in.
  • 2.4L turbo, lives in the XT and Wilderness trims, 260 hp, ~24 mpg combined, tows 3,500 pounds.
  • Wilderness for 2026 picks up electronically controlled dampers, you'll feel them the first time you point it down a washboard road off NV 157.
  • Ground clearance: 8.7 inches standard, 9.5 inches on Wilderness. More than a 2026 RAV4 (8.1 in) or CR V (8.2 in).

That extra inch matters more than people think when there's an inch of slush at the Lee Canyon trailhead.

04The Forester

2026 Forester. The family pick.

If you've got kids in car seats, this is the conversation we have with you, and it usually ends with you in a Forester.

The roofline is squared off and the rear doors open wide. Sounds boring on paper. Try installing two car seats in a Forester and a Crosstrek back to back and you'll get it. Cargo with the seats folded is 74.4 cubic feet. Ground clearance is 8.7 inches, or 9.3 on the Wilderness, newly increased for 2026.

The base 2.5L Boxer makes 180 hp, 178 lb ft and runs up to 29 mpg combined. The story for 2026 is the Forester Hybrid: same 2.5L Boxer paired with electric motors, 194 combined hp, 35 mpg combined (35 city, 34 highway), and around 581 miles per tank. For Las Vegas stop and go between I 15 and Charleston Boulevard, the regen braking pays you back every commute.

05The Crosstrek

2026 Crosstrek. Small, lifted, capable.

This is the most purchased first Subaru for buyers in their twenties and thirties at our store.

Right answer for the Henderson to Strip commute, the Summerlin commute, the southwest valley commute. Wrong answer if you've got two kids in car seats, get a Forester. Wrong answer if you tow a side by side, get an Ascent. Right answer for almost everything else when you're starting out.

One thing changed for 2026 that's worth flagging: the 2.0L is gone. Every gas Crosstrek now has the 2.5L Boxer at 180 hp, including Base. So if you're looking at a used 2024 or 2025 Base trim and wondering why the new one feels different, that's why. Combined fuel economy is 29 mpg on standard trims, 27 mpg on Wilderness. Ground clearance is 8.7 inches, or 9.3 inches on Wilderness, enough for the unpaved roads to most southern Nevada trailheads. Cargo behind the second row is 19.9 cubic feet, 54.7 with the seats folded.

The new Crosstrek Hybrid runs in Sport Hybrid and Limited Hybrid trims, 194 combined hp, up to about 36 mpg city. If your commute is mostly stop and go and you've got the budget to step up from the gas Crosstrek, it pays itself back faster than people expect at Vegas gas prices.

Towing: 1,500 lb. Small camping trailer, two mountain bikes on a hitch carrier, yes. Side by side, no. Current pricing: browse Crosstrek inventory · Crosstrek Hybrid: browse Crosstrek Hybrid inventory.

06Ascent & Solterra

2026 Ascent. The three row.

The Ascent is Subaru's biggest SUV and the only three row.

Eight passengers with the bench, seven with captain's chairs. The 2.4L turbo makes 260 hp, 277 lb ft and tows 5,000 pounds, most tow rating in the lineup, by a wide margin. Subaru.com lists max cargo at 75.6 cubic feet with both rear rows folded, plus underfloor storage. Ground clearance: 8.7 inches.

Who actually needs three rows

For Vegas, the Ascent earns its space when you're hauling four kids and a grandparent, or when you've got a side by side on a trailer headed to Logandale or Valley of Fire. The third row is genuinely usable for adults on a Sunday Mt Charleston run, that's the difference between this and a midsize three row from another brand where the back row is kids only.

If your third row sits empty most weeks, a Forester saves you money and parks easier. If it doesn't, the Ascent is the one.

Trims: Premium, Limited, Limited Bronze Edition, Touring, Onyx Edition Touring. Starts around $40,795 (Premium). Current pricing: browse Ascent inventory.

2026 Solterra, the all electric Subaru

The Solterra got a real upgrade for 2026, not a refresh, an upgrade. EPA estimated range is up to 288 miles, which is a meaningful jump from the prior generation. The new battery and charging system go from 10% to 80% in about 28 minutes at up to 150 kW on a DC fast charger. The Solterra now uses the NACS port, so it plugs into the Tesla Supercharger network. Battery preconditioning is new, which matters less in Vegas than it would in Reno.

Standard Solterra makes 233 combined hp with dual electric motors. The new Solterra XT trims push that to 338 hp and a sub five second 0 to 60. Ground clearance is 8.3 inches with standard Symmetrical AWD and dual function X MODE. That last part is the Solterra's whole pitch in one sentence: it's the only EV in this segment with a Subaru AWD calibration and X MODE underneath it.

Solterra vs. Tesla Model Y or Mach E, in plain English

For a Las Vegas commute pattern with a Mt Charleston weekend mixed in, you're well inside one charge. Home charging on NV Energy off peak is cheap relative to gas. Where the Solterra still trails Tesla and Rivian is on long cross country runs, if that's your use case, look at the Trailseeker or wait another model year. 07Which Subaru fits your week?

Frequently asked questions about the 2026 Subaru lineup.

How many SUVs does Subaru sell in 2026?

Nine, if you count the hybrids and EVs separately: Crosstrek, Crosstrek Hybrid, Forester, Forester Hybrid, Outback, Ascent, Solterra, Trailseeker, Uncharted. Five core gas SUVs, two hybrids, two EVs.

Every gas Subaru ships with Symmetrical AWD standard. The Solterra and Trailseeker are dual motor AWD by EV platform design.

Which Subaru SUV has the most cargo space?

The Outback carries the most among the non three row models, up to 80.5 cubic feet with the rear seats folded after its 2026 redesign.

The Ascent, Subaru's biggest SUV, carries up to 75.6 cubic feet with both rear rows folded plus underfloor storage. Forester is 74.4 cu ft, Solterra 63.5, Crosstrek 54.7.

Which Subaru SUV tows the most?

The Ascent at 5,000 pounds. After that: Outback XT and Outback Wilderness at 3,500 lb, Forester Wilderness at 3,500 lb, Solterra and Trailseeker at 3,500 lb each.

Other Forester trims tow 1,500 lb. Crosstrek tows 1,500 lb.

Which Subaru SUV gets the best fuel economy?

Among gas only models, Forester and Crosstrek tie at 29 mpg combined. The Forester Hybrid is 35 mpg combined. The Crosstrek Hybrid is up to about 36 mpg city.

On the EV side, the Solterra ranges up to 288 miles on a charge.

Is the Solterra worth it compared to a Tesla Model Y or Ford Mustang Mach E?

For a Vegas commute with weekend Mt Charleston trips, the Solterra holds its own. Subaru engineered Symmetrical AWD with X MODE, same X MODE as the Forester Wilderness and Outback Wilderness, 8.3 inches of ground clearance, and dealer service through our Subaru certified techs. The 2026 refresh closed most of the previous range and charging gaps: 288 mile range, 150 kW DC fast charging, NACS port.

For long cross country EV runs, the Tesla still has the edge on charging network density and the Mach E has more range on the GT trim. For day to day Vegas use, the Solterra is the more capable AWD vehicle.

What's the right first Subaru for a college student?

The Crosstrek, almost always. Lowest starting price in the SUV lineup, 29 mpg combined, AWD standard, and 8.7 inches of ground clearance for the unpaved roads to most southern Nevada trailheads.

New grads should ask about the Subaru College Grad Program through Subaru Motors Finance, see our College Grad Program page.

Which Subaru fits a family of four with car seats?

The Forester or Forester Hybrid. Squared roofline, easy car seat install, 74.4 cu ft cargo, 35 mpg on the hybrid. Try installing two car seats in a Forester and a Crosstrek back to back and you'll get it.

If your week includes more than four humans on a regular basis, jump to the Ascent for the third row and 5,000 lb tow.
